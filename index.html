Frequently asked

Everything we get asked on the phone.

Does Medicare actually cover HBOT?

Yes, for any of the 14 FDA-approved indications. We verify your benefits before your first session and handle pre-authorization in-house.

Hard chamber vs. soft chamber - what's the difference?

Hard chambers reach 2.0–2.8 ATA with 100% medical-grade oxygen. Soft chambers cap at 1.4 ATA and 31% O₂, far below clinical dosage. We use only hard chambers.

How long does each session take?

About two hours total: 90 minutes inside the chamber plus 15 minutes of pressurization and 15 of decompression. Most patients watch a movie or sleep.

Is HBOT safe? What are the risks?

HBOT is one of the most studied therapies in medicine. The most common side effects are mild ear-pressure and temporary near-vision changes, both reversible.

Can I bring my own prescription?

Absolutely. If you don't have one, our medical director can issue a prescription after your free consult.

How many sessions will I need?

Most protocols range from 20 to 60 sessions. We map your full plan during the consult so you know what to expect.

Can I bring a family member into the chamber room?

Yes. A loved one can sit beside the chamber for the entire session. We have a comfortable companion area in the treatment room.

Do you treat off-label conditions like long COVID?

We do, under self-pay or CareCredit. Long COVID, TBI, fibromyalgia and certain neurological cases are common off-label protocols here.
